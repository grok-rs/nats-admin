## Explore your data

- **Key-Value**: keys are grouped by `.` into folders. Open a value as a document; JSON is pretty-printed, and saving is conflict-safe and live-reloading. The **KV Browser** adds a structure analysis of all values, history diffs and charts.
- **Streams**: the **Stream Dashboard** shows throughput, limits, subject distribution and consumer lag. The **Message Browser** pages through stored messages; the **Subjects** node shows message counts per subject.
- **Consumers**: use **Consumer Lab** to fetch messages and ack / nak / term them.
- **Topology**: one graph of subjects → streams → consumers, mirrors and services.
