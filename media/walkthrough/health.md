## Check health

**Run Health Check** analyses streams, consumers, buckets, the JetStream account and the server against NATS best practices. It flags unbounded streams, limits nearly reached, single replicas in a cluster, consumers falling behind or stalled at max ack pending, poison-message loops, old servers and more.

Problems show up in the **Health** view, on the affected tree items and in the status bar, each with an explanation and a suggested fix.
